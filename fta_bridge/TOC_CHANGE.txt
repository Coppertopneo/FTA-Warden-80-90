Follow The Arrow TOC change
===========================

Do NOT redistribute the full Follow The Arrow addon from this repository.

Add this line to FollowTheArrow/FollowTheArrow.toc after Core\GuideArrow.lua:

Core\WGGBridge.lua

FollowTheArrow/Core/WGGBridge.lua
